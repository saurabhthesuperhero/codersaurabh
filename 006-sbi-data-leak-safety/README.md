# SBI data leak: stay safe from fake "SBI" calls 🛡️

Everything from my Short. This page is for **everyone**, so share it with your family.

## What happened (Oct 2026)
- **16 Oct:** a hacker claimed on a dark-web forum to be selling SBI customer data. SBI's threat-intel partner and **CERT-In** (India's government cyber-security agency) flagged it the same day.
- **21 Oct:** SBI told the stock exchanges that about 3.75 lakh records were checked and **3,897 customers' personal data was confirmed**.
- **Leaked:** name, date of birth, mobile number, email, address, branch/CIF IDs, **loan details**.
- **NOT leaked:** passwords, PINs, OTPs, card details. SBI says *"Customer balances are completely safe."*
- **Source of the leak:** not disclosed yet. An independent investigation is under way.

## ⚠️ The real risk: scam calls that know your details
Scammers can read your own details back to you to sound like the bank, then say:
- "Your loan is **pre-approved**, pay a processing fee"
- "Your **KYC is pending**, your account will be blocked"
- "Install this app / **APK**"

…and finally they ask for your **OTP**.

## ✅ 3 rules
1. **SBI never asks for an OTP, PIN, CVV or password.** Cut the call and call SBI yourself on an official number.
2. **Never install apps from SMS or WhatsApp links or APK files.** Use **YONO SBI** only from the Play Store or App Store.
3. **Lost money?** Call **1930** right away and file a complaint at **[cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/)**.
