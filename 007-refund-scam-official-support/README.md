# Refund scam: where to find REAL customer support 🛡️

Everything from my Short "₹35 ka dahi, ₹98,000 gaye". Share it with your family.

## What happened (Bengaluru, Oct 2026)
A 56-year-old woman wanted a refund for a leaking Blinkit curd packet. She searched online for "Blinkit customer care number" and called the number she found. The "support agent" sent a link on WhatsApp. She opened it and typed her password, the app vanished from her screen, and **₹98,000 was debited** from her bank account.

## ❌ The 3 mistakes
1. **Searched Google for a customer care number.** Scammers post fake "customer care" numbers on Google, Maps and fake websites, and sometimes they show up at the top.
2. **Opened a link from WhatsApp.** These links are often a fake app (APK) that hides itself and silently reads your SMS, OTPs included.
3. **Typed a password.** Game over.

## ✅ 3 rules
1. **Get customer care only from inside the official app** (Help / Support), never from a Google search.
2. **No real company asks for an app install, link, password or UPI PIN to give a refund.** Refunds go back to your original payment method automatically.
3. **Lost money?** Call **1930** right away and file at **[cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/)**. The sooner you complain, the better the chance the money can be stopped.

## 📱 Official support: where it actually is
| App | Where to find support (no phone call needed) |
|---|---|
| **Blinkit** | App → **Profile → Customer Support / FAQs**, or open the order → **Need help? → Chat** |
| **Zomato** | App → **Profile → Help / Support** → choose the order → **Chat** |
| **Swiggy** | App → **Account → Help** → choose the order → **Chat** |
| **Amazon** | App → **☰ Menu → Customer Service**, or https://www.amazon.in/contact-us |
| **Flipkart** | App → **Account → Help Center**, or https://www.flipkart.com/helpcentre |

These apps support you **inside the app**. If someone gives you a "customer care" number from Google and then sends a link or asks for a PIN, it's a scam.

## 🏦 Banks
- Use **only** the number printed on the **back of your debit/credit card**, or the one inside your bank's **official app**.
- **SBI:** 1800 1234 · 1800 2100 (toll-free)
- Your bank will **never** ask for an OTP, PIN, CVV or password over a call.

## 📵 Report fake numbers, calls and SMS
- **Sanchar Saathi – Chakshu:** https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/sfc/
- **Consumer complaints (unresolved refunds):** National Consumer Helpline **1915** · https://consumerhelpline.gov.in
